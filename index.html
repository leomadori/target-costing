Target Cost Calculator

Market-into-company target costing, from customer weightings to the control chart

INPUT 1MARKET DATA

INPUT 2FUNCTION–COMPONENT MATRIX

Rows are product functions with their customer weighting, columns are components with their drifting cost. Each cell says how many % of a function a component delivers; every row should total 100.

STEP 1TARGET COSTS

STEP 2BENEFIT SHARES

Benefit share of a component = Σ (share of function × customer weighting).

STEP 3TARGET COST INDICES

Target cost index = share of benefit ÷ share of costs. Allowable cost of a component = share of benefit × allowable cost per unit.

STEP 4CONTROL CHARTS

Target cost zone between Y1 = √(x² − q²) and Y2 = √(x² + q²). Above the zone: cost reduction needed. Below the zone: room for higher value. What next? See how target costs are achieved.

Standard: cost share = drifting cost ÷ total drifting costs

Extended: cost share = drifting cost ÷ allowable costs per unit

Method after Seidenschwarz (1993) · example "Grape SE – gMobile 5G" from the Management Accounting reader, ch. 3
